Online Safety Tips

Follow these simple steps to better protect yourself online.

  • Use strong and unique passwords for each account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication whenever possible.
  • Do not click suspicious links in emails or messages.
  • Keep your phone, apps, and computer updated.
  • Use secure Wi-Fi and avoid entering private information on public networks.
