Everyday safety tips

Use these steps to better protect your accounts, devices, and information.

  • Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts.
  • Enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links in emails or messages.
  • Keep your apps, phone, and computer updated.
  • Use secure Wi-Fi before entering personal information.
