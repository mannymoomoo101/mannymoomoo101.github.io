Top safety tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication when possible.
  • Do not click suspicious email or message links.
  • Keep devices and apps updated.
  • Use secure Wi-Fi before entering private information.
  • Sign out of shared or public computers.
