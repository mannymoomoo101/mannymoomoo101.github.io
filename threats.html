COMMON ONLINE RISKS

Threats you should know about

These are some of the most common cybersecurity risks people face online.

See How to Stay Safer
📩

Phishing

Phishing uses fake messages or websites to trick people into giving away passwords, banking details, or personal information.

💻

Malware

Malware is harmful software that can damage devices, steal information, or track activity.

🔓

Weak Passwords

Weak or reused passwords make it much easier for attackers to access accounts.

📶

Public Wi-Fi Risks

Unsecured public networks can expose your information if you log in to sensitive accounts without protection.

Threat comparison table

Examples of common online threats and how to reduce the risk
Threat What it does How to reduce risk
Phishing Tricks users into sharing private information Check senders, avoid suspicious links, verify websites
Malware Damages devices or steals data Keep software updated and avoid unknown downloads
Weak Passwords Makes accounts easier to break into Use longer, unique passwords and password managers
Public Wi-Fi Can expose activity on unsecured networks Avoid sensitive logins or use trusted protection
