COMMON ONLINE RISKS

Threats you should know about

These are some of the most common online dangers people face today.

📩

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites that try to steal private information.

💻

Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices, spy on activity, or steal data.

🔓

Weak Passwords

Easy-to-guess passwords make accounts much easier to break into.
