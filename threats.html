Common Online Threats

These are some of the most common dangers people face online.

Phishing

Phishing uses fake emails or messages to trick people into giving away passwords or personal information.

Malware

Malware is harmful software that can damage a device, spy on users, or steal data.

Weak Passwords

Short or easy passwords make it easier for attackers to break into accounts.
