Common Online Threats

These are some of the most common dangers people face when using the internet.

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites designed to trick users into giving away private information.

Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices, steal data, or monitor activity.

Weak Passwords

Short or easy-to-guess passwords make accounts much easier to break into.
